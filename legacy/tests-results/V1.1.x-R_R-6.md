# V1.1.x-R · R-6 — Remove `aura_python` CPython embedding (PRD §4.1/§8 복구)

**Date**: 2026-04-17 (Session #2)
**Scope**: full removal of the `aura_python` surface area. Language-policy restoration — PRD §4.1 prohibits interpreter languages and §8 line 203 lists "Python/스크립트 플러그인 인터페이스" as Out of Scope, yet Task 1.7 shipped a CPython embedding. Tasks.md 1.7 `[x]` is being reclassified REMOVED.

## Decision

Delete the entire `aura_python` component (library, header, test, GUI Script Manager menu action) and the CMake wiring. The feature was never exercised by the CLI/GUI product path and conflicts with the "C/C++ only" foundation documented in `CLAUDE.md`.
